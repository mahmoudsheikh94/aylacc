## Project Overview

This loft conversion project transformed unused attic space into a beautiful master bedroom suite with en-suite bathroom, significantly increasing the property's living space and value.

## The Challenge

The clients needed additional bedroom space for their growing family. The existing loft had excellent potential but required careful planning to maximise usable floor area while meeting building regulations.

## Our Solution

We designed and constructed a dormer loft conversion featuring:

- Rear dormer extension for maximum headroom
- New staircase integrated seamlessly with existing layout
- Spacious master bedroom with built-in storage
- Modern en-suite bathroom with premium fixtures
- Velux windows for natural light
- Full insulation and climate control

## The Result

The completed loft conversion has provided the family with a stunning master suite that feels like a natural part of the home. The additional space has transformed their daily living while adding significant value to the property.
