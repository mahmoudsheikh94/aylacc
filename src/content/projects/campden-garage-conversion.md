## Project Overview

This garage conversion project transformed an existing garage into a high-quality living space, adding valuable square footage to the property without the need for an extension.

## The Challenge

The client wanted to convert their attached garage into usable living space while ensuring the conversion met all building regulations and seamlessly integrated with the existing home.

## Our Solution

We delivered a comprehensive garage conversion including:

- Structural modifications for door and window openings
- Full insulation to meet current thermal standards
- New flooring raised to match internal levels
- Electrical and heating installation
- Plastering and interior finishing
- External render to match existing property

## The Result

The finished conversion provides a versatile additional room that feels like a natural part of the home, adding both living space and property value.
