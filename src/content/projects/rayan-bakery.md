## Project Overview

This bakery fit-out transformed an empty commercial unit into a fully operational bakery with retail frontage, combining production space with an attractive customer-facing area.

## The Challenge

The project required creating both a functional commercial baking area meeting all food safety regulations and an appealing retail space that would attract customers and showcase products.

## Our Solution

Our comprehensive fit-out included:

- Professional bakery kitchen installation
- Display counter and retail area design
- Ventilation and extraction systems
- Food-safe wall and floor finishes
- Customer seating area
- Full electrical installation for commercial equipment
- Signage and branding integration

## The Result

Rayan Bakery now operates from a beautiful, purpose-built space that efficiently supports production while providing customers with a welcoming environment to enjoy freshly baked goods.
