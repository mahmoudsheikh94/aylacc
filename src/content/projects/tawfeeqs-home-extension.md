## Project Overview

A substantial rear extension project that has transformed this family home by creating a spacious open-plan kitchen and living area, perfect for entertaining and everyday family life.

## The Challenge

The existing kitchen was cramped and disconnected from the garden. The clients wanted to create a modern, open-plan living space that would flow seamlessly into their outdoor area.

## Our Solution

We delivered a comprehensive extension including:

- Structural steel frame for open-plan design
- Large sliding doors connecting inside to outside
- Contemporary kitchen installation
- Underfloor heating throughout
- Bi-fold doors to the garden
- Matching exterior finishes

## The Result

The completed extension has revolutionised how the family uses their home. The bright, open-plan space has become the heart of the house, providing room for cooking, dining, and relaxation while maintaining a strong connection with the garden.
