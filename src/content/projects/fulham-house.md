## Project Overview

A comprehensive renovation project that transformed this Fulham property while preserving its period features and enhancing its functionality for modern family living.

## The Challenge

The property required extensive renovation work including structural repairs, complete rewiring, new plumbing, and interior refurbishment while maintaining its Victorian character.

## Our Solution

Our team delivered a complete renovation including:

- Structural repairs and reinforcement
- Full electrical rewiring to current standards
- New plumbing and heating system installation
- Kitchen and bathroom refurbishment
- Restoration of original period features
- Modern insulation for improved energy efficiency

## The Result

The finished property beautifully balances period charm with contemporary comfort, providing the owners with a home that respects its heritage while meeting the demands of modern living.
