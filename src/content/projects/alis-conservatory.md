## Project Overview

This conservatory project transformed an underutilised outdoor area into a beautiful, light-filled living space that seamlessly connects the home to the garden.

## The Challenge

The client wanted to extend their living space with a conservatory that would provide year-round usability while maintaining the aesthetic character of their property.

## Our Solution

We designed and constructed a bespoke conservatory featuring:

- High-quality aluminium framing for durability and slim sightlines
- Energy-efficient double glazing throughout
- Underfloor heating for year-round comfort
- Bi-fold doors opening onto the garden
- Matching roofing materials to blend with the existing property

## The Result

The completed conservatory has become the heart of the home, providing a versatile space for relaxation, dining, and entertaining guests while enjoying views of the garden in all seasons.
